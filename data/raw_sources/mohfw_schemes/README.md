### Why this content is critical

Government scheme information directly answers many common questions:
- "Am I eligible for free treatment?"
- "How do I register for Ayushman Bharat?"
- "Where can I get free teleconsultation?"
- "What is ABHA/health ID?"

Including this content allows SwasthyaSetu AI to guide users toward
government entitlements they may not be aware of.

After adding PDFs, run `python backend/ingest.py` to process them.
