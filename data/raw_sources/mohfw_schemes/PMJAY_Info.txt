Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY) is a flagship scheme of the Government of India, being implemented by the National Health Authority (NHA).

Benefits of Ayushman Bharat PMJAY scheme:
- Provides health cover of Rs. 5 lakhs per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization across public and private empanelled hospitals in India.
- Cashless and paperless access to services for the beneficiary at the point of service.
- Covers up to 3 days of pre-hospitalization and 15 days of post-hospitalization expenses such as diagnostics and medicines.
- No restriction on family size, age or gender.
- All pre-existing conditions are covered from day one.

How to register / Eligibility for Ayushman Bharat:
- PM-JAY is an entitlement-based scheme. There is no formal enrollment process or registration required.
- Beneficiaries are identified based on the deprivation and occupational criteria of the Socio-Economic Caste Census 2011 (SECC 2011).
- Eligible families are given an Ayushman card.
- To check if you are eligible, you can visit the official PM-JAY website (mera.pmjay.gov.in), call the national helpline 14555, or visit any empanelled hospital or Common Service Centre (CSC) with your ration card or Aadhaar card.
- Once verified, a PMJAY e-card (Ayushman Card) will be issued to you which can be used to avail free treatment.
