About SwasthyaSetu AI

A RAG-based multilingual health information assistant built for Indian citizens. Powered by advanced AI — designed with safety, accessibility, and trust as core principles.

Start Chatting →

⚠️ Critical Disclaimer

SwasthyaSetu AI is a health INFORMATION and EDUCATION tool, NOT a medical diagnostic system. It is designed to provide general health awareness and help users understand publicly available health information from WHO, ICMR, and the National Health Portal of India.

This tool should never be used to:

  • Diagnose any medical condition
  • Determine specific medication dosages
  • Replace consultation with a qualified healthcare professional
  • Make emergency medical decisions (always call 108 for emergencies)

What SwasthyaSetu AI Does

SwasthyaSetu (meaning "health bridge" in Sanskrit) connects Indian citizens with reliable, multilingual health information from authoritative government and international health sources.

  • Answers health questions in 10+ Indian languages (Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi, and English)
  • Provides information about disease symptoms, prevention strategies, and general health guidance
  • Explains government health schemes (Ayushman Bharat, PMJAY, eSanjeevani, Jan Aushadhi)
  • Detects emergency situations and immediately provides helpline numbers (108, KIRAN, Women's Helpline)
  • Supports voice input and output in Indian languages via Bhashini (Government of India)
  • Helps locate nearby hospitals and Primary Health Centres (PHCs)

🛡️ Safety Guardrails

Every interaction is protected by multiple layers of safety mechanisms:

🚨
Emergency Detection (Rule-Based)

A deterministic keyword matching system scans every query BEFORE any AI processing. If emergency keywords are detected (chest pain, suicidal ideation, severe bleeding, etc.), the system immediately returns helpline numbers WITHOUT calling the AI model — ensuring the fastest possible safety response.

🚫
No Dosage Information

The AI is explicitly instructed to NEVER provide specific medication dosages, drug quantities, or treatment schedules. Users are always redirected to consult a qualified doctor or pharmacist.

📚
Knowledge Boundary Enforcement

The AI is instructed to use ONLY information from the retrieved health knowledge base (WHO, ICMR, NHP India). When information is insufficient, it explicitly says so instead of generating unsupported information.

⚕️
Mandatory Disclaimers

Every AI response includes a reminder that the information is general and not a medical diagnosis. A permanent disclaimer banner is always visible on the chat interface.

💙
Mental Health Sensitivity

The system detects expressions of mental health crisis or suicidal ideation and responds with compassion and the KIRAN Helpline (1800-599-0019), not with AI-generated mental health advice.

Key Design Choices

  • BAAI/bge-m3 embeddings: Enables cross-lingual retrieval — a Hindi query can match English source documents without translation, because both are embedded in the same semantic vector space.
  • Deterministic emergency detection: Rule-based matching (no LLM) for emergency keywords — auditable, instant, and fail-safe.
  • RAG architecture: Grounds all responses in retrieved documents, preventing hallucination and ensuring factual accuracy.
  • openai/whisper-large-v3 (HuggingFace): Multilingual STT that handles Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali and other Indian languages without a dedicated ASR per language.
  • facebook/mms-tts (HuggingFace): MMS (Massively Multilingual Speech) TTS covers 22+ Indian languages with per-language model endpoints.
  • Light healthcare UI: Deliberately chosen over dark mode — healthcare UX research shows light interfaces build more trust for medical information.
